Support

HistoryPal keeps photos on your device, asks you to confirm recognized text, and saves completed study material locally.

Camera or photo question

  • Allow Camera or Photos access when iOS asks, or change access in Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Use a clear, well-lit image with the question fully visible.
  • Review and edit the recognized words before tapping the answer button. Choosing another image or cancelling does not use quota.

AI answer unavailable

  • Confirm that the iPhone has a working internet connection.
  • Use a specific history question of 10–700 characters.
  • If a request times out, fails, or reports insufficient evidence, it does not consume successful-answer quota. Wait briefly before retrying a service error.
  • Device verification requires a physical supported Apple device; it does not work as production proof in the simulator.

Purchases and restore

  • Plan availability and prices come from the App Store and can vary by region.
  • Open HistoryPal Settings and choose Restore Purchases after reinstalling or changing devices.
  • Confirm that the same Apple Account is active in the App Store. Apple manages billing, cancellation, refunds, and purchase history.
  • Saved answers remain available even when premium expires.

Contact

Email ppejic89@gmail.com with the subject “HistoryPal Support”. Include the app version, iOS version, what you expected, and the visible error message. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, Apple Account credentials, DeviceCheck tokens, receipts, transaction data, or a student’s personal information.

Support requests are handled in English or Serbian. HistoryPal has no phone support.
