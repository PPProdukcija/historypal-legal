Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

This policy explains how HistoryPal: Homework Helper (“HistoryPal”) handles information when you use the app.

Photos and text recognition

Camera and photo-library access occurs only after you choose those actions and grant Apple’s permission. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device. You can review and edit the recognized words before sending a question. The selected photo is discarded after confirmation or when you leave the flow and is never uploaded to HistoryPal’s server or to an AI provider.

Questions and AI answers

When you confirm a typed or recognized question, the question text, app locale, reading level, a random request identifier, and limited capability flags are sent over HTTPS to a PPProdukcija-operated backend. The backend sends the confirmed question to OpenAI to produce a structured answer and may use OpenAI web search to identify sources. The backend validates the response and source coverage before returning it. Do not include personal information in a homework question.

HistoryPal does not use questions or generated answers for advertising. The backend does not store raw questions, OCR text, generated answers, source queries, or provider response bodies.

Device verification and quota

Apple DeviceCheck is used to confirm that requests come from a genuine Apple device and to reduce quota abuse. The raw DeviceCheck token is sent to Apple for validation and is not stored or logged. The backend derives a pseudonymous, app-specific installation hash and stores only that hash with request identifiers, timestamps, quota tier, outcome, and coarse cost accounting needed to enforce the free quota, premium fair-use limit, and service budget.

Purchases

Subscriptions and payments are handled by Apple through StoreKit. HistoryPal does not receive payment-card details or your Apple Account credentials. When premium access is used, the app may send an Apple-signed transaction and a device-verification identifier to the backend. The backend verifies Apple’s signature, product, bundle, environment, expiration, refund or revocation, upgrade state, and device binding. Signed transactions and transaction identifiers are not logged or stored.

Saved history

Generated answers, timelines, quiz progress, and saved history are stored locally on your device. They are not synchronized to a HistoryPal account or server. Removing the app may remove this local data. Expiration or cancellation of a subscription does not hide or delete previously saved content.

Operational security and retention

Server logs are limited to a request identifier, controlled result or error code, coarse duration, source-count and remaining-quota buckets, and framework latency metadata. Request bodies, authorization tokens, IP addresses in analytics, and content are not logged. Network infrastructure may process an IP address transiently for delivery and rate limiting. Quota ledger records are retained only as needed to enforce access limits, prevent replay, investigate abuse, and meet operational or legal requirements.

Third parties

  • Apple provides DeviceCheck, StoreKit, App Store distribution, and optional Apple diagnostics under Apple’s own terms.
  • OpenAI processes confirmed question text to generate and source-check an answer under PPProdukcija’s service configuration.
  • Hosting providers process encrypted network traffic and operate the backend and database on PPProdukcija’s behalf.

Children and schools

HistoryPal is a general-audience study aid and is not designed to collect a child’s name, email address, school, or other profile information. The app has no account system, advertising identifier, or social features. Parents, guardians, and schools should supervise use as appropriate and should not ask learners to enter personal information.

Your choices

You can deny camera or photo-library access and type a question instead. You can stop before confirming OCR text. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings and delete locally saved history in the app. For privacy questions or a request concerning support correspondence, email ppejic89@gmail.com.

Changes

If HistoryPal’s data practices materially change, this policy and relevant App Store disclosures will be updated before or when the change takes effect.
